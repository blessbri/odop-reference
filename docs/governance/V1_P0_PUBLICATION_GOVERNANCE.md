# V1-P0 Publication Status, Provenance, Compatibility, Archival, and Maintenance Record

**Status:** Planning draft — no Version 1 supporting publication exists yet.

## Publication status

No JSON representation, JSON Schema, registry entry, fixture, or profile is
approved, published, frozen, or implementation-authoritative at V1-P0. A future
supporting publication must have its own stable identity, version, status,
publication date when issued, applicable Protocol version, and archival location
(Protocol 10.1–10.5, 24.3, 34.1, 34.9).

## Provenance and authority

Every future normative representation/schema statement must cite its exact
frozen Protocol source. Protocol citations are the sole basis for protocol
meaning. Architecture citations identify implementation responsibilities only
(Architecture Chapters 1–6).

## Compatibility

A future representation/schema publication must state its compatibility
relationship to earlier material. Later supporting material cannot silently
change the meaning of an earlier artifact, representation, profile, or registry
reference (Protocol 10.1, 10.5–10.7, 24.2, 26.4, 34.3–34.6).

## Archival

When published, prior representation/schema versions and the associated
supporting material must remain available or archivally preserved enough to
interpret historical artifacts. This record does not choose an archive system,
persistent identifier, or publication platform (Protocol 10.1, 21.2, 26.4,
31.1–31.6, 34.3).

## Maintenance

A representation or schema update is a separately versioned supporting
publication only if it remains within the core boundary. A proposed change to
core meaning requires Protocol maintenance rather than a representation-only
update (Protocol 34.2, 34.4–34.7). Publication governance details remain
unresolved and are not selected by this record (26.8–26.9, 33.10, 34.10–34.11).
